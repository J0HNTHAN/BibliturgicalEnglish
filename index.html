Reading — THE LORD'S PRAYER (Listen to Track 1)

Our Father, who are (art) in heaven, hallowed be Your (Thy) name. Your (Thy) kingdom come. Your (Thy) will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil (through Jesus Christ Our Lord). For Yours (Thine) is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, forever and ever. Amen.

شرح مبسط: كل جملة معناها إيه؟
Our Father … hallowed be Your name

يا أبانا اللي في السماء، اسمك يتقدّس. (hallowed = مقدَّس)

Your kingdom come

ليأتِ ملكوتك: نطلب إن الله يملك على حياتنا وعلى العالم.

Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven

تكون مشيئتك على الأرض زي ما هي في السماء: نطلب إن إرادة الله تتم عندنا زي ما الملائكة بتنفّذها.

Give us this day our daily bread

أعطنا خبزنا كفافنا اليوم: نطلب احتياجنا لليوم ده بس، ونثق إن الله هيرزقنا بكرة كمان.

Forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us

اغفر لنا خطايانا زي ما إحنا بنغفر لمن أخطأ إلينا. (trespass = يتعدّى/يخطئ)

Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil

لا تُدخلنا في تجربة، لكن نجّنا من الشرير (من الشر).

لأن لك الملك والقوة والمجد إلى الأبد. آمين = يكون كذلك.

معلومة: كلمات زي art / Thy / Thine هي إنجليزي قديم. معناها: art = are، Thy = Your، Thine = Yours. عشان كده الكتاب حاطهم بين قوسين.
Answer the following questions
1. How do we begin our prayers? (The sign of the cross)
الإجابة

We begin our prayers with the sign of the cross.

2. What is the meaning of (on earth as it is in heaven)?
الإجابة

It means we ask God that His will be done on earth in the same way it is done in heaven, perfectly, by the angels.

يعني: نطلب إن إرادة الله تتنفّذ هنا زي ما بتتنفّذ في السماء.

3. What is the use of repeating (day) and (daily)?
الإجابة

It stresses that we need God's care every single day, so we ask for what we need today and trust Him for tomorrow.

التكرار للتأكيد: this day = النهارده، وdaily = كل يوم. معناه إننا محتاجين الله كل يوم.

4. We ask God the Father to forgive our sins if we ..... (Complete)
الإجابة

…if we forgive those who trespass against us.

يعني: لو إحنا بنسامح اللي أخطأ إلينا.

5. What is the meaning of (evil)?
الإجابة

Evil means everything that is wicked and against God: sin and the work of the devil.

الشر: كل حاجة وحشة ضد الله، زي الخطية وعمل إبليس.

4. Explain Solomon's "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge."
الإجابة

It means that respecting God and obeying Him is the first step to real knowledge and wisdom. Without it, a person cannot truly learn.

«مخافة الرب» هنا مش رعب، لكن احترام الله ومحبته وطاعته. ده أول طريق الحكمة.

Language — B. Nouns (1): Singular and Plural (المفرد والجمع)
Tip — من الكتاب
  • The usual way to form the plural: -s: book/s, car/s, seat/s, day/s, boy/s, key/s
  • -es: (nouns ending in –s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z) bus/es, wish/es, box/es.
  • consonant + y → consonant + ies: country→countries, party→parties, baby→babies.
  • nouns ending in –f → ves: half→halves, life→lives, self→selves, shelf→shelves, wife→wives. except: beliefs, chiefs, roofs.
  • There are some irregular noun plurals: man→men, woman→women, child→children, foot→feet.
  • There are nouns which do not change in the plural: fish, deer, sheep.
  • There are nouns which look singular but are in fact plural: cattle, clergy, people, police.
  • There are nouns ending in –s but they are in fact singular: News, measles, billiards, ethics, economics, physics, politics.

Nouns (1) — 2. Countable and Uncountable Nouns
Tip — من الكتاب
  • Countable nouns are for things we can count using numbers. They have a singular and a plural form. The singular form can use the determiner "a" or "an". If you want to ask about the quantity of a countable noun, you ask "How many?" combined with the plural countable noun.
  • Uncountable nouns are for the things that we cannot count with numbers. They may be the names for abstract ideas or qualities or for physical objects that are too small to be counted (liquids, powders, gases, etc.). Uncountable nouns are used with a singular verb. They usually do not have a plural form. For example: tea, sugar, water, air, rice, knowledge, beauty, anger, fear, love, money, research, safety, evidence.

إجابات مقترحة

١) المثل (Parable) قصة قصيرة من الحياة اليومية بتحمل معنى روحي. ٢) استخدم الرب الأمثال عشان يبسّط التعليم، ويخلّي الناس تتذكره، ويكشف الحق للي عايز يفهم.

Reading — THE PRODIGAL SON [1] (Listen to Track 2)

11 Then He said: "A certain man had two sons.
12 "And the younger of them said to his father, 'Father, give me the portion of goods that falls to me.' So he divided to them his livelihood.
13 "And not many days after, the younger son gathered all together, journeyed to a far country, and there wasted his possessions with prodigal living.
14 "But when he had spent all, there arose a severe famine in that land, and he began to be in want.
15 "Then he went and joined himself to a citizen of that country, and he sent him into his fields to feed swine.
16 "And he would gladly have filled his stomach with the pods that the swine ate, and no one gave him anything.
17 "But when he came to himself, he said, 'How many of my father's hired servants have bread enough and to spare, and I perish with hunger!
18 'I will arise and go to my father, and will say to him, "Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you,
19 "and I am no longer worthy to be called your son. Make me like one of your hired servants." '

(Luke 15)
القصة باختصار: ولد صغير أخد نصيبه من أبوه وسافر بعيد وبدّد كل فلوسه. جات مجاعة، واشتغل يرعى خنازير وكان جعان. فرجع لنفسه وقرر يرجع لأبوه ويعترف.
Answer the following questions
1. What did the younger son ask his father? Why?
الإجابة

He asked his father to give him the portion of goods that fell to him, because he wanted to travel away and live as he liked.

2. Where did the prodigal son spend his money? How?
الإجابة

He spent it in a far country, and he wasted it with prodigal living (carelessly).

3. What is the job he found after losing his money?
الإجابة

He worked as a swine feeder (keeper): a citizen of that country sent him into his fields to feed swine.

4. Compare between "Give me my portion of goods", and "no one gave him anything."
الإجابة

At first his father gave him a lot of good things. After he went away from his father and wasted everything, no one gave him anything.

المقارنة: وهو مع أبوه كان بياخد ويتعطي بسخاء، ولما بعد عن أبوه وخسر كل حاجة، محدش أعطاه ولا حاجة.

5. Why did the younger son decide to go back to his father?
الإجابة

Because his father's hired servants had bread enough and to spare, while he was perishing with hunger.

Tip — من الكتاب
  • A/An: This is used when the noun that we wish to refer to is unknown to our listener/reader or is not part of the common ground that we share. It is most often used to introduce new information.
  • The: By using (the), we are telling our listener that he knows what we are referring to and/or that the context of our conversation should help them to identify this. We can use (the), therefore, to: refer backwards to something that we have already mentioned; refer forwards to something that we can take for granted will happen; refer to our common ground or shared knowledge.

The present simple tense is used:
